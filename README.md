# Singles.rs ❤️🦀  

**The dating hub for single developers!**  
The only community **by Rust developers, for Rust developers,** and exclusively **Rust developers** looking for relationships and dating worldwide.  

## 🌍 Who is this for?  
- **Rustaceans** 🦀 who are single and looking for meaningful connections.  
- Developers who love Rust and want a partner who shares their passion for programming.  
- Anyone in the Rust community interested in dating or meeting like-minded people.  

## 🚀 How to Get Started  
1. **Introduce yourself** – Open an issue with a short bio, your interests, and what you’re looking for.  
2. **Browse** – Check out other issues to find potential matches.  
3. **Connect** – If you find someone interesting, reach out via GitHub discussions, social media, or private messages.  

## 📜 Rules & Guidelines  
- Keep it respectful and professional.  
- No spam or inappropriate content.  
- This is a Rust community-focused dating hub—other languages are cool, but Rust is our common ground.  

## ❤️ Why Rust?  
Because **memory safety is sexy,** and we all love a good **safe abstraction** in our relationships.
